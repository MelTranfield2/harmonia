A needle sweeps each map from left to right. Each slice plays one note: its pitch is the value in that slice and it comes from the left or right speaker to match the slice's position. A low drone follows the summary number for the frame and gets rougher the further it moves from the first frame. A soft drum marks each new frame.

Imagery: NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). Datasets: MERRA-2 (GMAO), SSMIS sea ice concentration (NSIDC), MODIS Terra NDVI.
