How the image becomes sound

  • Brighter image → more treble: higher notes and a brighter tone.
  • Colours close together → more harmonic: a plain chord. Varied colours add extra notes, and very mixed colours clash.
  • Warm colours (red, orange, yellow) → major chord. Cold colours (blue, cyan) → minor chord.
  • Left / right → each half of the image plays in its own speaker.
